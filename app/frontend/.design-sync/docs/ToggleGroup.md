A row of `Toggle` chips for multi-choice (`type="multiple"`) or single-choice (`type="single"`) settings. Compose with `ToggleGroupItem` children, each with a `value` and a label: `<ToggleGroup type="multiple" defaultValue={["en"]}><ToggleGroupItem value="en">English</ToggleGroupItem>…</ToggleGroup>`. `size` on the group applies to every item.
