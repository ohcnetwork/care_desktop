Label + optional "i" explainer + control + messages under it. Props: `label`, `htmlFor`, `info` (content shown in an info Alert when the round "i" button is pressed), `infoTitle`, `messages` (12.5px muted lines under the control; colour individual lines with `text-danger-ink` / `text-brand-ink`), `children` (the control). `InfoButton` (props `onClick`, `pressed`, `title`) is the standalone round "i".
