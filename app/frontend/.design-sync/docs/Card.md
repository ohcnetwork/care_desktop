White rounded-xl card with a `line` border and `shadow-card`. It has no padding of its own: add it (`className="p-5"` or `"flex flex-col gap-3 p-6"`). `CardTitle` (14.5px semibold) and `CardDescription` (13px muted) are the heading pair. Section kicker style used on cards: `text-[11.5px] font-bold tracking-[0.06em] text-muted-foreground uppercase`. For the dark hero card use a plain div: `rounded-xl bg-brand-deep p-5 text-brand-bg` with `white`/`glass` buttons.
