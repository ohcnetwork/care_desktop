The page frame to the right of the app sidebar: a full-height column. Compose `Screen` > `ScreenHead` (props `kicker`, `title`, `subtitle`, `onBack`, `backDisabled`; 24px bold title, optional uppercase green kicker, optional ghost Back button) > `ScreenBody` (scrolling content, 34px side padding) > `ScreenFoot` (white footer bar with a top border for the main buttons) with an optional `FootNote` (muted sentence after the buttons).
